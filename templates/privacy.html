How we log activity

PUR Media Library — security and audit only

We keep a technical activity log so the library stays secure and we can see who uploaded, downloaded or changed files. This is not advertising, profiling or marketing analytics.

What we collect

  • The email of the signed-in account (staff or portal client)
  • The type of action (login, logout, upload, download, delete, folder or portal changes, page load)
  • Technical details tied to that action (file name, page path, duration, portal name)
  • Date and time

We do not sell this data. In production, the same events may also be sent to Microsoft Azure Application Insights for technical monitoring.

Why

Security, accountability and the proper operation of the service (legitimate interest / performance of the service).

How long

Activity logs in the database are kept for 6 months, then deleted automatically.

Who can see it

Internal administrators and moderators only (Stats page). Portal clients cannot see other people’s logs.

Your rights

Under GDPR you may request access, rectification, restriction, objection or erasure, within the limits that apply to security logs. Contact PUR’s DPO or legal team, or a Media Library administrator. You may also lodge a complaint with the CNIL.
